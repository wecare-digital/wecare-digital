## Handset steps

1. From the QA handset, open WhatsApp's conversation with the first business number.
   Use WhatsApp's voice-call control, not the phone's cellular dialer.
2. Record start time in IST to the second, target number, ringing/answer outcome,
   whether actual in-call audio is audible, and whether the expected menu plays.
   A WhatsApp follow-up text does not count as in-call audio.
3. If a menu prompts for DTMF, try only a benign menu/navigation option. Do not
   initiate purchases, payments or a transfer to a real customer/operator.
4. Hang up after roughly 20–30 seconds. Record audio clarity, disconnect behavior,
   and any post-call notification. Repeat once for the second business number.
5. Supply the two timestamps and audio observations. Correlate CloudWatch/Lightsail
   and provider call metadata for that exact window; do not infer results from an
   empty table alone. Avoid collecting caller PII or SIP passwords in reports.

## Decision after evidence

- Keep the current instance: no fixed-price saving.
- Downsize micro to nano: $2/month list-price saving, requiring a tested rebuild
  and cutover; Lightsail is not an in-place bundle resize. Keep rollback until QA.
- Retire after a proven Plivo media replacement: up to $7/month AWS bundle saving,
  less any provider/number/media costs. India termination feasibility and the
  claimed Plivo per-minute rate have not been verified by this preparation.
- Disable SIP and retire: up to $7/month bundle saving, with an explicit product
  decision to lose in-call audio. Do not select this based solely on zero records.

Neither retirement option has been chosen or executed. Credits, data transfer,
snapshots, static IPs and provider costs can change actual cash savings.

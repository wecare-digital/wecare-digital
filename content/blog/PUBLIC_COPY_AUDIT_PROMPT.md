# WECARE.DIGITAL Blog Public-Copy Audit Prompt

Use this rule for all current and future WECARE.DIGITAL blog audits and publication checks.

## Public-copy rule

Public blog copy must not expose external source/reference material.

Remove or rewrite any public-facing occurrence of:
- named source authors, teachers, philosophers, public figures, chefs, researchers, or other people when they identify the source/provenance;
- named source works such as books, films, articles, studies, publications, organizations, or named teachings when they function as source/reference material;
- quotations that depend on external attribution;
- URLs, citations, reference labels, source labels, "according to...", "adapted from...", "the source...", "the cookbook...", "the book says...", or similar attribution language;
- source provenance, publisher names, author names, publication titles, institution names, or private source-biography details.

Internal provenance may remain in backend/source-profile metadata. It must not appear in public title, slug-derived presentation, SEO title, meta description, excerpt, or body copy.

Legitimate culinary/cultural identity may remain when it is genuinely part of the subject itself rather than source provenance.

## Audit behavior

- Do not delete posts to satisfy this rule.
- Preserve IDs, slugs, URLs, and original publication dates unless separately approved.
- Rewrite only the minimum necessary public text.
- Keep factual, health/safety, privacy, duplication, SEO, formatting, and structural checks active.
- Treat this as a publication blocker, not an attribution-review exception.
- No public attribution exception may bypass this rule.
- Corpus preservation floor: Conversations 976 + Gastronomy 454 = 1,430 records.

## Future-publishing behavior

All newly created or revised blog posts must pass this public-copy exclusion rule before publication.
